AeroStats Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the AeroStats application (“the application”), available on the App Store and Google Play.

1. Data controller

The application is operated by Cesar Machado. For any questions about your personal data, you can write to: crmm15@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account and email: if you create an account within the application, your email address is used only to sign in and recover your account.
  • Content you generate: sports data and statistics you save within the application (for example, athletes, routines, scores, elements, competitions, and recorded progress).
  • Media: photos and videos you upload to the gallery for athletes and competitions, with your device’s permission.
  • Underage athletes’ data: if an athlete is a minor, we process their data only with the consent of their tutor or representative, declared when registering them.
  • Purchases: AeroStats is a 100% free application: it has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not process payments of any kind.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is used to: (a) provide and maintain the functionality of the application; and (b) sync your content across devices. The legal basis is the consent you grant by using the application.

4. Third parties and external services

The application uses the following external services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — authentication, database, and storage. Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Apple / App Store — application distribution. Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google / Google Play — application distribution (if you use the application on Android). Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Storage and security

Data is stored on Supabase servers with encryption in transit. We adopt reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information and limit access to strictly necessary personnel.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all your data directly from the application (Menu → Delete account) or by sending us an email.

7. Your rights

Depending on your country, you may exercise the rights of access, rectification, deletion, portability, and objection to the processing of your data. To exercise them, write to crmm15@gmail.com and we will respond within the applicable legal timeframes.

8. Minors

The application records the competitive history of athletes, including minors. Accounts are created by adults (adult athletes, coaches, or representatives) and an underage athlete’s data is only processed when a tutor declares it when registering them. We do not use minors’ data for advertising purposes or sell it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy. If we make relevant changes, we will notify you within the application or by email. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or about the processing of your data, write to us at: crmm15@gmail.com.
